BOUNCY privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

BOUNCY is a place to watch and share videos. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who we are

BOUNCY is run by [add your full name or company name]. Contact us at [add your contact email] with any privacy question or request.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored in scrambled form, never in plain text), name, username, bio and profile photo.
  • What you post: videos, captions and comments.
  • What you do: likes, follows, blocks, and reports you send us.
  • Basic technical data our hosting provider processes to run the service, such as your IP address.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or payment details, and we do not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

To run BOUNCY: sign you in, show your videos and profile to other people, show you videos, keep the community safe, and respond to reports. We do not sell your personal information.

What other people can see

Your username, name, bio, photo, videos, comments and like counts are visible to anyone who opens BOUNCY, including people without an account. Your email address is not shown to other users. Liking, commenting, following, messaging and posting need an account.

Where it is stored

Your information is stored with our service provider Supabase on servers in the United Kingdom. This means your information is transferred outside South Africa. Videos are stored in a public file storage area, so anyone who has a video’s direct link can open it.

Reports and safety

When you report a video or block someone, we keep that record to review the report and protect the community. We may remove content that breaks the law or our rules, and we may disclose information when the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can edit your username, bio and photo on your Profile page, and delete your own videos at any time.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile, tap Delete account, and enter your password. This permanently removes your profile, videos, comments, likes and follows. Copies in routine backups may remain for a limited time before they are overwritten.

Age

BOUNCY is for people aged 18 and older.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, contact us first. You can also complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator at inforeg.org.za.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
